Contract Roles Analysis

Inspect access-control roles of a smart contract on Ethereum, Base, Polygon, or Arbitrum — fully in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere except the RPC endpoint you provide.

1. Connection

2. Role-changing methods

The methods to look for in transaction history. Tick whether each should be treated as granting and/or revoking a role, and choose which argument is the role (bytes32) and which is the account (address).

3. Role constants (optional)

Map human-readable names to their bytes32 values. Compute keccak256(name) automatically or paste a raw value. Used to label results and to verify membership.

4. Membership check (optional)

The view method used to confirm whether an account currently holds a role. Optionally list extra candidate addresses (one per line) to check directly — useful for roles granted via internal calls the scan can't see.

5. Scan & analyze

A bounded block-range scan discovers candidate role holders by decoding the methods above (including Gnosis Safe execTransaction/multiSend wrappers), then hasRole confirms current membership.
